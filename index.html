Who we are

Nautical Logic provides Captain’s Tools (formerly Captain helper), an Android app for vessel GM estimates, ETA and speed planning, fuel comparisons, and manual rolling-period timing. This policy explains how the app and this privacy-policy page handle information. It applies to the app’s current features, including Google AdMob banner and app-open advertising and an optional Google Play Premium purchase. You do not need to create an account to use Captain’s Tools.

Vessel details and calculations

Vessel names and dimensions you save, ETA inputs, speed settings, fuel-consumption rates, and display preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device where the feature saves them. Other calculation entries and stopwatch measurements may be held only during the current session. These values support the calculations, saved vessel list, and your settings.

The app does not send these vessel records or calculation entries to a Nautical Logic server, and it does not explicitly pass them to Google as advertising parameters. There is no Nautical Logic cloud account or cloud-sync service. Captain’s Tools does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise device location. Manual timing uses your taps. If you start sensor-assisted measurement, the app reads rotation-vector sensor data to calculate roll and pitch angles and a dominant period using FFT. Recording runs only while the measurement screen is active and stays in memory unless you save a CSV. Imported recordings are analysed locally. CSV files contain elapsed times and roll/pitch angles and are saved to the location you choose; that storage provider may sync them to the cloud. Nautical Logic does not receive sensor readings or CSV contents.

Advertising and Google services

Captain’s Tools uses Google AdMob to show banner and app-open advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising privacy choices.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This includes your IP address (which can indicate general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Collection and ad personalisation depend on applicable choices, device settings, and service configuration. A test-ad label does not mean the SDK makes no network requests.

Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google processes service data under its own policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure. Nautical Logic does not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service in the current app.

Your advertising choices

Where required, the app uses Google’s consent message before requesting ads. An “Ad privacy choices” control is available in the app’s ad footer when Google requires that entry point. You can use it to review or change available choices. You can also manage the advertising ID and available ad privacy settings through Android settings; options vary by Android version.

Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all ads. You can continue using the calculation features if ads are unavailable. Ads are not displayed on the manual stopwatch or sensor-measurement screens. Premium removes all app ads.

Android backup, retention and deletion

The app allows Android’s backup and restore system. Depending on your phone and backup settings, Android or your device’s backup provider may back up and restore app data. Those backups are governed by your device settings and the backup provider’s policies.

Saved app data remains on your device until you remove the relevant saved record, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Session-only values are not a permanent measurement history. Clearing app storage removes the app’s local records and preferences; it does not automatically erase existing device backups or information already processed by Google. A later restore may bring back backed-up data.

Google controls retention of its advertising and consent information under its policies and applicable settings. Nautical Logic cannot promise deletion of data held independently by Google or your backup provider.

Premium purchases

Premium is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play that removes banner and app-open ads. Google handles payment details; Nautical Logic does not receive your payment-card details. The app receives product ID, purchase token, status, and a signed purchase record to verify ownership, acknowledge purchases and restore Premium. A local entitlement is kept for offline use and excluded from Android backup. Ownership is checked again with Google Play. Pending or unverified payments do not activate Premium. Purchase tokens are not uploaded to a Nautical Logic server. Google Play shows availability and price before confirmation.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, provide support, and address privacy questions. Email is handled through our email provider. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can ask us about deletion. Please avoid including confidential vessel records or unnecessary personal information in support messages.

Service providers and processing locations

The app communicates with Google for advertising and consent services. If you tap an advertisement, the advertiser’s website or app has its own privacy practices. Google, our email provider, the provider hosting this page, and any backup provider may process information in countries other than your own according to their policies. Nautical Logic does not sell your saved vessel records or calculation entries.

This policy webpage

This page contains no advertising, added analytics scripts, tracking pixels, contact forms, or embedded third-party media. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, and request logs to deliver and secure the page. Clicking an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy policy.

Privacy requests

Depending on where you live and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy authority. Contact Nautical Logic using the email below for information we handle directly. For Google advertising data, also use Google’s privacy controls and request channels; for backups, use your backup provider’s controls. We may ask for information needed to identify and respond to your request.
